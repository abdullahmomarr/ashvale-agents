Why agents

Somewhere in your business, the same job gets done by hand every week.

For one owner it is the quotes nobody chased. For another it is the content that never gets written, the report someone rebuilds every Monday, or the applications piling up in an inbox. Your team is busy with today's customers, so it waits.

An agent is software that does one of those jobs every day, the same way each time, and checks with a person before anything goes out. It works inside the tools you already use, so nobody has to learn a new system.

Fit

Who this is for, and who it is not for.

An agent is the wrong tool for some jobs. We would rather tell you that on the first call than build something you will not use.

A good fit

  • You have a team and tools already in use, like a CRM, Slack or a shared inbox.
  • There is a job that repeats every day or every week.
  • You can say what that job is worth in booked work, hours or missed follow ups.

Not a fit

  • The job comes up a handful of times a month.
  • It needs a fresh judgment call every time.
  • The way it gets done changes from week to week.

If that is your situation, we will say so on the audit call.

Questions

Before you book.

What kinds of agents do you build?

Whatever the audit turns up. So far that has meant sales follow up, call prep, content, finance briefs, hiring boards and customer assistants. If a job repeats and someone on your team can describe how they do it, we can usually build an agent for it.

Why is the first agent free?

Because you have no reason to trust us yet. We would rather show you one agent doing real work in your business than ask you to believe a pitch. If it works, we expect you will want the rest.

What happens when the 14 days are up?

If you want to continue, we build the rest of the plan. If you do not, we switch the agent off, part ways, and you owe nothing.

What does the full build cost?

It depends on what the audit finds, so we do not publish a single number. You see the price of every agent in your plan before you commit to anything.

Do you need access to our systems?

Yes, to the tools the agent works in, such as your CRM and Slack. We ask for the least access the job needs, and messages to your customers wait for a person on your team to approve them.

Which tools do you work with?

Slack, GoHighLevel, Close, Pipedrive, Typeform and Google Workspace so far. If your tool lets other software connect to it, we can usually work with it, and the audit call is where we check.
